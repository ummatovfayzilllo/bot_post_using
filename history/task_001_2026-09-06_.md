## 🛠 2. Amalga Oshirilgan Ishlar (Implementation)

1. **Konfiguratsiya va Xavfsizlik:**
   - [`src/global/user_filter/user_filter.guard.ts`](../src/global/user_filter/user_filter.guard.ts) yaratildi. Faqat `.env` da ko'rsatilgan `ALLOWED_USER_IDS` foydalanuvchilariga ruxsat beriladi.
2. **Ma'lumotlar Bazalari:**
   - PostgreSQL uchun [`prisma/schema.prisma`](../prisma/schema.prisma) tuzildi (`User`, `Channel`, `Group`, `Post`, `PostTarget`) va `npx prisma db push` orqali bazaga yuklandi.
   - MongoDB uchun [`PostBackupSchema`](../src/core/schemas/post-backup.schema.ts) va [`StateBackupSchema`](../src/core/schemas/state-backup.schema.ts) ulandi.
3. **State va Navbat Boshqaruvi:**
   - [`StateService`](../src/core/state.service.ts): In-Memory `Map` + Redis kesh + MongoDB fallback zaxirasi.
   - [`MessageQueueService`](../src/core/message_queue.service.ts): BullMQ orqali rejalashtirilgan (deadline) postlarni boshqarish va server qayta yoqilganda navbatni qayta tiklash (rehydration).
4. **Kanallar va Guruhlar Moduli:**
   - [`ChannelsService`](../src/modules/channels/channels.service.ts) va [`GroupsService`](../src/modules/groups/groups.service.ts) — ID / @username / havola orqali qo'shish va `getChatMember` orqali adminlik huquqini avtomatik tekshirish (Health Check).
5. **Postlar va Wizard Interfeysi:**
   - [`AdminUpdate`](../src/modules/admin/admin.update.ts) — `/start`, `/new_post`, `/posts`, `/channels`, `/groups`, `/cancel` buyruqlari, matn/media qabul qilish, inline tanlash va yuborish.
